About the book

Peace that does not wait for outcomes.

You’ve been told peace will come once things settle down. Once the bills are paid, the diagnosis is clear, the business turns around, or the marriage heals. But what if waiting for better circumstances is the very thing keeping you anxious? And what if comfort, not crisis, is the quieter danger to your faith?

Peace Over Comfort is for anyone carrying pressure they cannot fix, and for believers who have grown comfortable enough to stop needing God.

Through real stories of success, bankruptcy, betrayal, and loss, you will learn why comfort can never hold you, how a simple daily practice of closeness with God steadies you before anything changes, and how to catch fear and temptation in the first few seconds, before they take over. You will discover what it means to trust God in hard times and find a peace that does not wait for outcomes.

You do not have to wait for life to get easier to live in peace.

About the author

Daniel Todd

Daniel Todd has built companies that generated more than half a billion dollars in revenue, and he has been through bankruptcy twice. Somewhere between the two, he learned he had been more anxious with millions in the bank than he ever was with nothing.

Today he leads Influence Mobile, a four-time Inc. 5000 honoree and the company behind Rewarded Play. This book grew out of a series of messages he gave at his church during some of the hardest years his company has faced, including “Living in the Burn” and “Missing the Mark.” He wrote it for anyone standing in a fire they did not choose and for believers who have grown too comfortable to notice they need God.

Daniel serves on the boards of International Media Ministries and Summit Life Church. He and Katherine, his wife of thirty-four years, have two adult children and live in Sedona, Arizona, where much of this book was dictated on the trails they hike together each week.
